About me

Get to know me

I'm from Khameythang, Samdrup Jongkhar. I finished Class XII in Commerce at Desi High School, then followed my curiosity into technology. Today I study Computer System and Network at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College, where I learn enterprise networking, security, automation, backend development and web design.

Alongside my studies I've completed two CCNA courses through the Cisco Networking Academy, and online courses on Coursera in Linux server administration, Python GUI programming and full-stack web development.
